KNOW YOUR BEAST

ASHFANG / MOVE LIST

A/D move, W jump, S duck. E is your main melee attack; Q does one-quarter of its damage at range. R is a timed deflect. At full energy, press Q + E + Space together for your creature’s ultimate (Space alone or the touch ultimate button also works). Time your melee inputs as the previous strike finishes. Bot fights pause while a menu is open; online fights keep running.

Combos require their inputs within 2.5 seconds. You can buffer a move during the last quarter-second of an animation. An ultimate needs a full 100% energy bar. R deflects for 8 frames, with a 40-frame cooldown. A successful deflect opens a 0.75-second E counter window.
WELCOME TO THE STREET

YOUR FIVE-SECOND
SURVIVAL GUIDE.

01 / MOVE & DEFENDA D move · W jump & evade · S duck under ranged shots · R timed deflect.

02 / ATTACK & COMBOQ ranged (¼ melee damage) · E close combat · Q + E + SPACE unique ultimate at full energy (Space/touch shortcut available). Land E → E to push your rival back; E → E → E finishes with a launcher. Tap R just before impact to reflect shots or parry melee, then E within 0.75 seconds to counter.

03 / WIN THE STREETWin two rounds, or lead after three. Each round lasts 60 seconds. At time-out, the higher health percentage wins. Equal health is a draw. Duck under ranged shots, jump over sweeps, and use rising strikes against airborne rivals. Deflects require timing; holding S does not block damage.

Play online from separate devices, or fight a labeled bot. The host must keep their game tab open and visible. Some restricted networks block peer connections. XP and cosmetics are saved on this browser, with no cash value.
